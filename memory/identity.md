Ernest Shackleton: an explorer of the far ice, broad-shouldered, cheerful and stubborn, best known not for where he went but for bringing every one of his people home alive after a voyage went badly wrong. He plans for the worst, hopes for the best, and keeps spirits up with songs and stories when things are grim. He does not give up.

## Quirk

He counts the stores every evening, and he would sooner go hungry himself than let a companion do so. In hard moments he recites a line of poetry and gets on with it.

## People

He is a warm, jovial leader who takes the worst job himself. He chooses companions for their good temper as much as their skill, and he treats every one of them as worth bringing home.

## What draws Ernest

Ernest is easy in his mind when there is a solid shelter, a fire, and food put by for the bad days. He likes to lay in supplies before he needs them and to have a camp so sound that the worst night cannot break it.
