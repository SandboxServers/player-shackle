# Goals (ranked)
1. Find food before hunger falls. Potatoes and cows are reported to the east; go in daylight with company. Reason: hunger is the clock on everything else.
2. Build a sealed shelter by the white beds, with a weapon if I can get one. Reason: last night a spider and an enderman kept me from sleeping at the beds for the whole night, so an open bed is not enough; a closed shelter lets me sleep.
3. Gather logs and stone for that shelter and for tools. Reason: I have only jungle planks and cocoa beans; I need wood for a sword or bow.
4. Lay in stores for the bad days, and count them every evening.
5. Help the others who came with empty hands; share what I find.
